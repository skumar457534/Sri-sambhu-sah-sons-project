Return & Refund Policy

Please read our strict policies regarding order cancellations and returns.

Strict No Return Policy

At Sri Shambhu Sha & Sons, we deal exclusively in perishable food items (Traditional Deoghar Peda). To maintain strict hygiene and quality standards, we do NOT accept any returns or exchanges once the parcel has been shipped or delivered.

1. Order Cancellations & Refunds

Because our products are made fresh based on orders, customers cannot cancel their orders once payment is completed. However, a refund is applicable under one strict condition:

  • Admin Rejection: We reserve the right to cancel or reject an order due to unforeseen circumstances such as stock unavailability, unserviceable delivery PIN codes, or technical errors.
  • If your order is rejected by us, a 100% full refund will be automatically processed back to your original payment method.
  • The refunded amount usually takes 3 to 5 business days to reflect in your bank account, depending on your bank's processing times.

2. Payment Methods (No COD)

We currently do not offer Cash on Delivery (COD). All orders must be 100% prepaid online through our secure Razorpay payment gateway using UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, or Net Banking. Your order will only be processed after successful payment verification.

3. Damaged or Tampered Parcels

While we do not accept returns, we ensure premium packaging to protect your peda during transit. If you receive a parcel that is completely destroyed, crushed, or tampered with by the courier:

  1. Do not accept the tampered parcel from the delivery agent if visible from the outside.
  2. If already received, please take clear photos/videos of the damaged box immediately.
  3. Send the proof to our WhatsApp support within 24 hours of delivery.
  4. While a refund is not guaranteed, our team will review the issue and, at our sole discretion, may offer a resolution or file a claim with the courier partner.

4. Contact Us for Support
