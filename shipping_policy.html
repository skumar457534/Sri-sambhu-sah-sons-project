Shipping Policy

Information regarding delivery, tracking, and secure packaging.

1. Order Processing & Dispatch

At Sri Shambhu Sha & Sons, we understand that you eagerly await the authentic taste of Baba Dham Peda. Because our products are prepared fresh to maintain their purity and quality:

  • Most orders are prepared, securely packed, and dispatched within 1 to 2 business days after the payment is successfully verified.
  • Orders placed on weekends or national holidays will be processed on the next business day.
  • You will receive an email confirmation once your order is accepted and another one when it is shipped.

2. Shipping Partners & Timelines

To ensure fast and reliable delivery across India, we use premium third-party logistic aggregators (such as Shiprocket). This allows us to partner with top courier services like Blue Dart, Delhivery, XpressBees, and Amazon Shipping.

  • Estimated Delivery: During checkout, you will be shown estimated delivery dates based on your PIN code and the selected courier partner. Usually, delivery takes between 3 to 7 business days post-dispatch.
  • Delays: While we strive for timely deliveries, the estimated dates are strictly approximate. We are not legally liable for delays caused by external factors (extreme weather, natural disasters, courier strikes, or logistical issues on the carrier's end).

3. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges are calculated dynamically at the time of checkout. The cost depends entirely on the total weight of your cart and the delivery PIN code. Our system integrates directly with courier APIs to fetch real-time, transparent shipping rates so there are no hidden costs.

4. Live Order Tracking

Transparency is our priority. Once your package is handed over to the courier partner, an AWB (Air Waybill) tracking number is automatically generated and emailed to you. You can track your package's live location step-by-step using our Track Order page.

5. Packaging Security & Perishable Goods

Deoghar Peda is a perishable food item, and preserving its shape, freshness, and aroma during transit is vital. We utilize high-quality, food-grade packaging designed to withstand long-distance shipping.

Customer Notice: If you receive a package that looks completely crushed, tampered with, or open from the outside, please refuse to accept the delivery from the courier agent and contact our support immediately with photographic proof.

6. Non-Serviceable Areas

Our system checks your PIN code's serviceability at checkout. If your area is exceptionally remote and unserviceable by any of our courier partners, the checkout process will not proceed. If an order is somehow placed but cannot be shipped due to courier restrictions, we will reject the order and initiate a 100% full refund immediately.
