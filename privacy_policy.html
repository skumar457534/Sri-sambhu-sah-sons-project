Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Sri Shambhu Sha & Sons ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website (the "Site") and purchase our products.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when registering at the Site, expressing an interest in obtaining information about us or our products, or otherwise contacting us. The personal information that we collect depends on the context of your interactions with us and the Site, the choices you make, and the products and features you use.

  • Personal Details: Name, Email address, Phone number.
  • Delivery Information: Complete shipping and billing addresses, PIN codes, city, and state.
  • Account Data: Encrypted passwords (if you choose to create an account).
  • Payment Data: We do not store your credit card, debit card, or UPI details. All payments are processed securely through our trusted payment gateway partner, Razorpay.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information collected via our Site for a variety of business purposes described below:

  • To fulfill and manage your orders: We use your information to process payments, arrange for shipping via Shiprocket, and provide you with order tracking updates.
  • To communicate with you: We use your email and phone number to send you transactional emails (like order acceptance, shipment tracking, or refund notifications) and to respond to your customer service inquiries.
  • To manage user accounts: If you create an account, we use your data to manage your profile and order history.

4. How We Share Your Information

We only share information with your consent, to comply with laws, to provide you with services, to protect your rights, or to fulfill business obligations. We share your data with the following third parties strictly for operational purposes:

  • Payment Processors (Razorpay): To securely process your payments and refunds.
  • Logistics Partners (Shiprocket & Couriers): To generate shipping labels (AWB) and deliver your physical order to your address.
  • Communication Services (Brevo/WhatsApp): To send you automated order status emails or SMS notifications.
  • Hosting & Database (Firebase/Cloudflare): To securely store your order history and account details in our encrypted database.

5. Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal information. Our website uses SSL/HTTPS encryption to ensure that your data is transmitted securely. While we have taken reasonable steps to secure the personal information you provide to us, please be aware that despite our efforts, no security measures are perfect or impenetrable, and no method of data transmission can be guaranteed against any interception or other type of misuse.

6. Your Privacy Rights

If you have an account on our website, you can review or change the information in your account at any time by logging in. If you wish to have your account completely deleted along with your order history, please contact our support team.
